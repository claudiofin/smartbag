# SmartBag — tech pack

⚠️ **Generated by `tools/tech_pack.py` from `dimensions.py`, `hardware/bom.py`
and `cad/patterns.py`.** Do not edit this file; edit the design and run it again.
Everything below is derived, so a change to the bag reaches the person cutting
the hides instead of stopping at a CAD model.

⛔ **This is a first pattern, and that is how the trade works.** You cannot unroll
a 3D loft into a leather pattern and cut hides from it: a structured bag gets its
shape from the stiffener and the stitching as much as from the outline, leather
stretches on the bias and not across it, and every maker eases a curve
differently. Cut a toile in cheap material first. Two or three iterations before
the pattern is right is normal and none of them are a fault in this document.

## 3 · What the electronics require — these are specifications

### 3.1 ⛔ The charging window: Ø56 mm at the centre of the base

A **Ø48 mm** wireless charging coil sits at the exact centre of the
insert's underside, facing down through the base of the bag. Inside a
**Ø56 mm** circle centred on the base:

- ⛔ **no metal of any kind.** No feet, no rivets, no plate, no magnetic snaps,
  no metallised or foil-backed lining, no metal-cored stiffener.
- ⛔ **base thickness 3.5 mm and not more.** This is the gap between the
  two coils and it is already most of the budget: a standard pad works to about
  5 mm coil to coil, and 3.5 mm of leather plus the pad's own surface uses
  it up. A doubled base or a thick reinforcement here stops the bag charging and
  will look like an electronics fault.
- ✅ leather, fabric lining, non-woven stiffener, thread and glue are all fine.
  The field does not care about them.

⚠️ **Metal feet are standard practice on a bag this size and they are the single
most likely thing to go wrong.** If the bag needs feet, they go **outside** the
Ø56 mm circle and they should be non-metallic.

### 3.2 ⛔ The closure magnet wakes the product

The insert has a magnetic sensor in its collar: it is what tells the bag it has
been opened, and the entire power budget depends on it. Everything else is
asleep until the closure opens.

- the closure **must** carry a small permanent magnet;
- it has to pass within about **5 mm** of the insert collar when the bag shuts;
- ⛔ **one magnet, and no other magnets anywhere in the bag.** A magnetic
  pocket snap near the collar holds the product permanently awake, which shows
  up as a battery that lasts days instead of months;
- ⛔ **no magnet within Ø56 mm of the base centre** — see 3.1.

### 3.3 The insert pocket

The insert is a rigid tray that drops into the bag and sits on its inner floor.

| | |
|---|---|
| footprint | **225 × 78 mm**, R16 |
| height | **179.6 mm** |
| bag inner at the base | 233 × 88 mm |
| clearance | **4.0 mm** each side, **5.0 mm** front and back |

⚠️ **That clearance is small and it is where a lining goes.** A padded or quilted
lining on the lower walls does not leave room for the insert. Line the walls flat.

### 3.4 The cell is a lithium pouch

LP523450JU+PCM+JST PHR-3 70MM — 950 mAh, in the insert, not in the bag. It
never comes out and there is no user-accessible battery door.

- ⛔ nothing in the construction may compress the base of the insert;
- ⚠️ the finished bag ships with a cell in it, which is a **UN 3481** shipment
  and needs the paperwork. The cell itself is already **UN 38.3** and
  **IEC 62133-2** certified by its maker — that is on its datasheet — but the
  finished product is a separate declaration.

## 4 · What is still open on the bag

Everything in section 3 is settled. None of the following is:

- ⛔ **no maker.** No pellettiere has seen this, no sample has been sewn, no
  quote exists.
- ⛔ **no closure chosen.** The neck has to pinch shut and carry a magnet;
  whether that is a zip, a magnetic flap or a drawstring is a design decision
  nobody has taken, and it changes the neck pattern.
- ⛔ **no lining or stiffener specified.** Which stiffener a structured bag uses
  decides how the panels are cut, so this comes before the pattern is final.
- ⛔ **no hardware.** Handle fixings, feet if any, closure hardware.
- ⚠️ **the pattern has never been cut.** See the note at the top: expect two or
  three iterations with the maker.
